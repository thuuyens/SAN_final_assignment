Survey design object -> to work with the weights

zdroj:https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/tutorials/weighting.aspx Weights are created in NHANES to account for the complex survey design (including oversamplig), survey non-response and post-stratification adjustment to match total population counts in the USA. A sample weight is assigned to each sample person. It is a measure of the number of people in the population represented by that sample person. NHANES: You must use the weight of the smallest subpopulation that includes all the variables you want to include in your analysis. Z tech mensich souboru obsahují speciální vahy jenom ty chemikálie (EPHPP a SSPHTE a PHTHTE). EPHPP a PHTHTE maji stejne (WTSB2YR) a SSPHTE ma jiné (WTSSBH2Y). Nevim ktera z nich by se mela pouzit, ale nejmensi je dataset SSPHTE. v ukazkach pak na vsechny upravy pouzivaji uz jenom survey desing objekt na práci s weighted NHANES dataset -> vytvorit z celych dat a znovu stejne profiltrovat

PCA

V clanku (Association between co-exposure to phenols and phthalates mixture and infertility risk in women): Use PCA to deal with collinearity of chemicals -> get fewer independent components. Factors with eigen values higher than 1 were determined as the main components. Z 54 chemikalii se to zmensilo na 15 main components.
